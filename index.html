How do I use ARCSKILLER?

Copy the code, choose your items and enter it in the cart before placing your order. Check the discount and final total before payment. Check the current offer and eligibility in the ARC Store cart.

Does the code stack with my personal discount?

For eligible catalog items, entering a promo code replaces the personal discount; they do not stack. Level V weapon builds, Raider Loadouts and blueprint sets use their own pricing and do not receive this promo discount. The referral link still identifies the partner for these orders. Check the final total before payment.

Can I use my skill-tree build here?

Use your plan on arcraiderskill.com as a guide when choosing weapons and gear. This page does not automatically import or validate a skill-tree build.

Who handles my order?

ARC Store handles checkout, fulfilment and order support. ChaosEon introduces the store through this partner page.

Where can I check reviews and policies?

Customer reviews are shown on the store’s product pages. Read the delivery information, terms and refund policy before ordering. We do not promise a particular delivery time on this partner page.
